Maple Row Street Fair

Sat · Oct 24 11am — 8pm Maple Row, 3rd to 6th

One block, fifty-eight tables, and the whole neighborhood on its feet. Come hungry, leave with a tote full of things you did not know you needed.

01 Eat

Eat your way down the block

Twelve kitchens, one street, zero reservations. Everything is cooked in front of you, and the line moves faster than it looks.

  • Smoke & Pickle — brisket on rye, half-sour pickles
  • Nnamdi's Grill — suya skewers, charred onion
  • Little Oven — cardamom buns, still warm
  • The Corn Cart — esquites with lime and chili salt

02 Listen

Two stages, no headliner

Every act lives within six blocks of the fair. Free, all ages, and loud enough to hear from the pickle table.

  • 11:30Maple Row Recorder Club
  • 1:00Sadie & the Sidewalks
  • 3:00DJ Marisol, all-ages dance
  • 5:30The 6th Street Brass

03 Make

Makers, menders & oddities

Fifty-eight tables of things made by hand and things worth fixing. Cash and cards both welcome, haggling optional.

  • Mender's Row — knife sharpening, zipper rescue, bike tune-ups
  • Handmade — ceramics, indigo dye, letterpress cards
  • Kids' table — clay monsters, face paint, one very slow piñata

Bring a tote.
Bring a friend.

Free entry · Rain or shine · Step-free from 3rd to 6th
